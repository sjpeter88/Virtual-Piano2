Concert Grand — Owner's Manual & Shortcuts

1. The 3D Instrument & Camera Navigation

  • 88-Key Concert Grand (A0 to C8): Realistic ivory white keys and glossy ebony black keys raised and grouped in twos and threes inside a lacquered grand case.
  • Note Names & Middle C: Toggle note labels in the View menu. Middle C (C4) is marked with a gold badge, and active computer keyboard bindings appear in brackets (e.g. [A]).
  • Zoom & Scroll: Open the View menu to zoom in (down to 25% span for smaller screens) and scroll across the keyboard, or use your mouse wheel directly on the 3D scene (Wheel to scroll horizontally, Ctrl + Wheel to zoom).

2. Physical-Modeling AudioWorklet Sound Engine

  • Inharmonic Overtones & Unison Strings: Synthesized in real time inside a dedicated Web Audio AudioWorkletProcessor. Overtones follow the stiff-string inharmonicity law $f_n = n f_0 \sqrt{1 + B n^2}$, with fractionally detuned unison string pairs per partial.
  • Velocity Brightness & Hammer Felt Thump: Harder strikes excite brighter upper partials along with a subtle wooden hammer-felt thump. Higher harmonics decay faster than the fundamental so notes mellow naturally as they sustain.
  • Sustain Pedal & Sympathetic Resonance: Hold Spacebar (or click Sustain) to lift the dampers and engage a 4-comb sympathetic string resonance bank.
  • 36-Voice Polyphony, Room Reverb & Soft Limiter: Old voices fade out smoothly without clicks, and a stereo FDN/Schroeder room reverb feeds a tanh soft limiter so dense two-hand chords never clip.

4. Chords, Scale Highlighter & Metronome

  • Chord Buttons (C, G, D, Am, Em, F, Dm, E7): Click or hold any chord button in the practice ribbon to hear the chord and see its exact keys depress and light up on the 3D piano.
  • Scale Highlighter: In the Practice menu, choose any root note and scale mode to illuminate matching scale degrees across all 88 keys (gold for root notes, blue for scale degrees).
  • Metronome: Toggle the metronome from the practice bar and adjust BPM (40–208) and time signature in the Practice menu.
